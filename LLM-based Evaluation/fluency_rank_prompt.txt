Evaluate the fluency of the following five candidate captions and rank them based on the evaluation results.

**Fluency:** Analyze the linguistic quality of each candidate and compare them to determine their level of fluency. Focus on three aspects: sentence structure, grammatical correctness, and overall coherence. ### User Prompt:

## Evaluation Criteria:

1. **Sentence Structure:** Assess whether the sentence is complete, well-organized, and structurally reasonable. Consider whether its components are arranged appropriately and whether the sentence is easy to understand.

2. **Grammatical Correctness:** Evaluate the correct usage of words, tenses, and grammatical structures. Identify grammatical errors, inappropriate word usage, and incorrect sentence constructions.

3. **Overall Coherence:** Determine whether the sentence flows smoothly and presents its information in a clear and logically coherent manner. Assess whether the ideas are naturally connected and easy to follow.

## Instructions:

1. Analyze each candidate independently against the three evaluation criteria.
2. Evaluate the sentence structure, grammatical correctness, and overall coherence of each candidate.
3. Compare the candidates to determine their relative fluency. Candidates with more complete sentence structures, fewer grammatical errors, and smoother, more coherent expressions should receive better rankings.
4. Do not reward candidates merely for being longer or containing more descriptive information. Focus exclusively on linguistic fluency rather than factual accuracy or descriptive detail.
5. Assign a unique rank to each candidate, from 1 (most fluent) to 5 (least fluent). Each rank must be used exactly once.
